New in Dime 3.0.1

• Multiple currencies: tap the currency symbol to log in any currency. Totals are converted to your primary currency at each day's rate.
• Quick Add: save up to 4 templates in Settings, then log them with one tap from the new Quick Add widget, without opening Dime.
• Upcoming charge reminders: get a heads-up before a recurring expense is added to your log.
• New Expense button for Control Center, the Lock Screen and the Action Button.
• Category is now optional: log a transaction without one, and it shows as Expense or Income.
• Start your week on any day.
• Export a period: choose the dates to include before exporting.
• Unlimited expense categories, in the order you set.
• Search by amount, even with a currency symbol or separators.
• Dime is now available in French and Russian.

Fixes
• You can now add a category even if the emoji keyboard isn't turned on.
• Typing a note on iPhone Duo no longer moves the add screen.
• Small layout fixes in Settings.
