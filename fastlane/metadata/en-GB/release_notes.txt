New in Dime 3.0.3

• Dime is now available in 15 more languages: Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia, Urdu, Indonesian, Malay, Vietnamese and Thai.
• On iPhone Duo, the buttons in sheets now sit in the side bar.

Fixes
• Fixed crashes on the first launch after updating.
• If Dime can't open your data, it now explains what happened and lets you try again instead of crashing. Your entries stay safe.
• Dime now tells you when a change can't be saved, for example when your iPhone is out of storage, and tries again later.
• Your entries no longer look missing when you're signed out of iCloud with sync turned on.
• Fixed a crash when a category was deleted while you were editing a transaction.
• Fixed crashes and freezes in Budgets, Insights, the log and the emoji keyboard.
